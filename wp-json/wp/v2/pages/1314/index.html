      \u0422ecnologia is responsive by design. We partner with organizations that appreciate the value of following a standards-based approach to inform the architecture and lifecycle management of their IT systems.<\/p>

      This alignment allows clients to enjoy business optimizing technology that doesn\u2019t require constant, reactive, emergency intervention \u2013 so they have more time to focus on growing their business.<\/p>

      We give users direct access to support, which eliminates bottlenecks and allows us to collect data and insights to identify training gaps and recommend system improvements.<\/p>

      Our Service Level Agreement prioritizes client matters and response times by P1, P2, P3, and P4. A significant server or cloud application outage is a P1.<\/p>

      Assisting with a password reset is a P4. Good news: we can set you up with a self-serve password solution, so you don\u2019t have to open a ticket.<\/p>

      The outcome is similar to the concept of compound interest. When you invest in the process over time, both quality of service and responsiveness accrue. Failure to invest (or starting late) makes it impossible to catch up.<\/p><\/div>\n\t\t\t\t<\/div>\n\t\t\t\t\t\t\t
